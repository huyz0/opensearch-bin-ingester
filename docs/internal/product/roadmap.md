## ⚠️ Decided by ADR-0052

M7's milestone review proposed an assembly milestone between M7 and M8.
[ADR-0052](decisions/0052-m8-owns-assembly-and-the-first-real-backend-because-its-evidence-is-unbuyable-without-them.md)
decided it the other way and says why: a separate milestone's completion
condition would have to be *"the thing starts"*, which nothing checks until
something breaks it — and the thing that breaks it is M8's chaos matrix. Of the
mechanisms wired to nothing, **most are only observable under a fault**,
so an assembly milestone would ship them with the same "met at the class"
evidence M7 had to write. M8 owns assembly instead, as its first tasks.
